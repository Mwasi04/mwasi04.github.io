Who We Are

A Legacy of Academic Excellence

Founded in 2003, Success Secondary School has grown into one of Lusaka's most respected secondary institutions. For over two decades we have delivered a rigorous, values-driven education that prepares students for university and for life.

Our school community is built on mutual respect, high expectations, and the belief that every child has the potential to achieve greatness. We blend strong academics with sport, arts, and community service to develop well-rounded citizens.
